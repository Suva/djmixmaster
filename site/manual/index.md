# The manual

DJ Mix Master masters recorded DJ mixes. Open a recording of a set or a whole night, mark the mixes inside it, and it evens out the loudness from track to track: louder tracks come down, quieter ones come up, and the breakdowns keep their dip. It can also make a video of each mix for YouTube or social media, moving to the music.

![The window with two mixes marked in a recording of a night](pictures/window-mastering.png)

## In five steps

1. **Open a recording.** Drop it on the window, or use Open recording…. See [Opening a recording](recording.md).
2. **Mark the mixes.** The whole recording starts as one mix; drag its edges, or split it at the silences.
3. **Listen.** Press Space to play, and A to hear the recording as it was. See [Listening and levelling](levelling.md).
4. **Adjust if needed.** The defaults suit most mixes.
5. **Export** the sound, or a video. See [Exporting audio](export-audio.md) and [Exporting video](export-video.md).

Your recording is never changed, and it never leaves your computer.

## In the app

Help → User Manual, or the **?** button, opens this manual in a window of its own, for the version you have, without a network. Its toolbar goes back, forward and home. Its search looks through every page: pick a result to go to that part of its page, where the words are marked. Links to the website open in your browser.
