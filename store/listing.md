## Description

(Up to 10,000 characters.)

DJ Mix Master masters recorded DJ mixes. A recording of a night is rarely even: one DJ plays hotter than the next, a track comes in quieter than the one before, the levels drift as the night goes on. Turning the whole thing up or down does not help, and a compressor pumps the breakdowns up until they are as loud as the drops.

DJ Mix Master levels track against track instead of moment against moment. It follows how loud each track is at full energy and evens those out, slowly, the way you would ride a fader. A quieter passage shorter than a breakdown keeps the gain of the track around it, so breakdowns keep their dip, and the result still breathes.

MASTERING
• Open a recording of hours, in almost any format: MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, AIFF, Ogg, or the sound of a video. A recording split into several files by the recorder opens as one.
• Mark the mixes inside it: split a night at the silences between sets, or anywhere by hand.
• Listen to the result as you change the settings, through the same limiter the export uses, and switch back to the original with one key.
• Raise or lower any stretch by hand, fade in and out, and even out the bass between tracks if you like.
• A true-peak limiter keeps the output under the ceiling, and the loudness can be aimed at what you need: as loud as fits, streaming, podcast or broadcast.
• A check-up of the recording finds clipping, a quiet or silent channel, a channel out of phase, dropouts and hum, and puts the channels right with one click.

TRACKS AND CHAPTERS
• Mark where each track starts, or import the track list from your DJ software.
• Exports carry the tracks as chapters, and the list can be copied for YouTube, which turns it into chapters too.

VIDEOS
• YouTube will not take a mix as sound alone. Make a video of each mix, moving to the music: glitter that bursts on the kick, the spectrum round your logo, the track playing, your own photo, or the recording's own picture for a set that was filmed.
• Landscape for YouTube, vertical for phones, square for feeds.
• Make templates of your own in an editor with layers, effects, blends and layer styles, video clips, LUTs, and anything moving with the bass, the beat or the energy. Share them as files.

EXPORT
• WAV, FLAC, MP3 and AAC, with tags, or an MP4 video.
• Every mix of a night in one go.

YOUR WORK STAYS YOURS
Your recordings are never changed and never leave your computer. The app collects nothing, and has no accounts and no advertising. It is free and open-source software, under the GNU General Public License.

A manual, with pictures of every part of the app, is at https://dmm.clysuva.com/manual/

## System requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Memory: 8 GB recommended. Recordings are read as streams, so their length does not matter, but video exports need room.
- A graphics card that supports DirectX 11 helps video exports; without one they are slower.
