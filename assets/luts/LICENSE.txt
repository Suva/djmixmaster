The LUTs that come with DJ Mix Master
=====================================

Film looks (the groups "Film: black & white", "Film: colour negative",
"Film: slide" and "Film: instant")

  Made from Pat David's film emulation HaldCLUTs
  (https://patdavid.net/topics/film-emulation-presets/), as published in
  https://github.com/NatronGitHub/clut. They are licensed under the Creative
  Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and so are these tables.

  Changed from the originals: each HaldCLUT of 64 points a side was read off
  at 33 points a side, between its own points, and stored in the app's own
  format (scripts/make-luts.mjs in the app's sources).

  The trademarked names of films are there only to say which film stock a
  look is meant to approximate. Neither the app nor the authors of the looks
  are affiliated with or endorsed by the companies that own the trademarks.

Looks (the group "Looks")

  Made for DJ Mix Master, from the recipes in scripts/make-luts.mjs, and
  under the app's licence, the GNU General Public License version 3.
